## Featured Project

### 🏎️ [Formula Student Electronics Study](https://github.com/DH-J320/Formula-Student-Korea-Electronics-Study)

A personal technical study developed from a **team-wide Formula Student circuit study**, centered on BSPD safety electronics and the evolution from **25EVO to LEF-26**.

The repository documents:

- comparator and threshold circuits,
- open-collector vs push-pull logic,
- RC timing and dedicated timer ICs,
- sensor fault detection,
- shutdown / fail-safe reasoning,
- schematic walkthroughs,
- numerical and Falstad/CircuitJS models,
- verification questions and future measurement plans.

I do **not** present the team hardware as my own design.  
The goal is to show how I move from a real engineering schematic to **system understanding, quantitative checks, and testable questions**.
